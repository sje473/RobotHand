## 👍 Thumb Assembly Instructions

### Materials Needed
- Thumb  
- Thumb piece (anchor joint that attaches to the palm)  
- 3 pulleys (one located on the thumb piece)  
- Elastic band  
- Metal paper clips (>1 mm diameter)  
- Two M2 × 16 mm screws  
- One M2 × 10 mm screw  
- Nylon string (fishing line)  
- Screwdriver and cutters/pliers  

---

### 1. Install the Pulleys
1. Cut **three (3)** paper clip pins — two **8 mm** and one **12 mm** in length.  
   - The **first pulley** will be installed on the **thumb piece**, and the other **two** on the **thumb** itself.  
2. Feed each pin through its pulley once to remove any 3D printing roughness or tight tolerances.  
3. At each joint, position the pulley between the pin slots, then insert a pin so the pulley can rotate freely without sliding out.  
4. Confirm that all three pulleys rotate smoothly and that each pin fits snugly.

---

### 2. Attach the Thumb to the Thumb Piece
1. Once the pulleys are installed, align the thumb and thumb piece so that their pin holes line up.  
2. Insert an **M2 × 10 mm screw** through the holes — this screw acts as a **pin joint**.  
3. Do **not tighten** the screw completely; leave a slight gap so the thumb can rotate freely.

---

### 3. Mount the Thumb Piece to the Palm
1. Before threading the elastic band, attach the thumb piece to the **palm** using **two (2) M2 × 16 mm screws** from underneath the palm.  
2. Make sure the **pin slot on the thumb piece** faces toward the **center of the palm** — this ensures the correct motion and tension direction.

---

### 4. Install the Elastic Band
1. Position the elastic band inside the thumb so it runs through the pulleys.  
2. Begin at the **tip of the thumb**, where you’ll find a small side hole.  
   - Insert an **8 mm pin** through that hole and through the band loop to anchor it under tension.  
3. Tie your **nylon string** securely to the elastic band.  
4. Feed the string down through the thumb’s internal holes toward the palm.  
5. At the palm connection, insert the **12 mm pin** through the band’s end hole on the thumb piece.  
   - This creates the pre-tension that makes the thumb return upright after bending.

---

### 5. Verify Motion
- Gently move the thumb to ensure:  
  - The band stretches and retracts with good tension, (not too strong or too weak)
  - The thumb naturally returns to its rest position.  

---

**Tip:** Start with moderate tension on the band — you can fine-tune it by swapping the elastic or adjusting pin positions later.
