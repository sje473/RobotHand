## 👍 Thumb Assembly Instructions

### Overview
Like the fingers, the thumb is printed as a **print-in-place mechanism**, using built-in pin joints for its motion.  
The **pulleys** are added to reduce friction for the actuation string that will later drive the thumb movement.

---

### Materials Needed
- Thumb  
- Thumb piece (anchor joint that attaches to the palm)  
- 3 pulleys (one located on the thumb piece)  
- Elastic band  
- Metal paper clips (>1 mm diameter)  
- Two M2 × 16 mm screws  
- One M2 × 10 mm screw  
- Nylon string (fishing line)  
- Screwdriver and cutters/pliers  

---

### 1. Install the Pulleys
1. Cut **three (3)** paper clip pins — two **8 mm** and one **12 mm** in length.  
   - The **first pulley** will be installed on the **thumb piece**, and the other **two** on the **thumb**.  
2. Feed each pin through its pulley once to remove any 3D printing roughness.  
3. At each joint, position the pulley between the pin slots, then insert the pin so it can spin freely but stay in place.  
4. Verify that all pulleys rotate smoothly — they’ll later guide the actuation string to minimize friction during motion.

---

### 2. Attach the Thumb to the Thumb Piece
1. Once pulleys are installed, align the thumb and thumb piece so the pin holes line up.  
2. Insert an **M2 × 10 mm screw** through the holes — this acts as a **pin joint**.  
3. Do **not fully tighten**; leave a slight gap for free rotation.

---

### 3. Mount the Thumb Piece to the Palm
1. Before threading the elastic band, attach the thumb piece to the **palm** using **two (2) M2 × 16 mm screws** from underneath the palm.  
2. Ensure the **pin slot on the thumb piece** faces toward the **center of the palm** for proper range of motion and band routing.

---

### 4. Install the Elastic Band
1. Position the elastic band inside the thumb so it follows the pulley path.  
2. At the **tip**, locate the small side hole.  
   - Insert an **8 mm pin** through that hole and through the elastic band to anchor it under tension.  
3. Tie the **nylon string** securely to the elastic band.  
4. Feed the string down through the thumb’s internal holes toward the palm.  
5. At the palm, insert the **12 mm pin** through the band’s end hole on the thumb piece.  
   - This creates pre-tension, allowing the thumb to spring back to its neutral position when released.

---

### 5. Verify Motion
- Manually bend and release the thumb to ensure:
  - The pulleys rotate freely.  
  - The band stretches evenly.  
  - The thumb returns upright without sticking.

---

**Tip:** Apply a small amount of lubricant to the pulley axles if movement feels rough — this keeps future string motion smooth and low-friction.
