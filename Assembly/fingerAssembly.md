## 🖐️ Finger Assembly Instructions

### Overview
The finger is designed as a **print-in-place mechanism**, meaning the joints are already functional when printed.  
Its motion relies on **built-in pin joints** for rotation, while the **pulleys** are installed afterward to reduce friction once the actuation string is added later.

---

### Materials Needed
- Metal paper clips (>1 mm diameter)  
- Rubber bands (elastic)  
- Nylon string (fishing line)  
- Wire cutters or pliers  

---

### Steps

1. **Prepare the Pins**  
   - Cut **four (4)** paper clip sections to **8 mm** length.  
   - Cut **one (1)** section to **12 mm** length.  

2. **Deburr the Pulley Holes**  
   - Feed each 8 mm pin through the pulleys to clean up any 3D printing residue or tight tolerances.  
   - Remove the pins once the pulleys rotate freely.

3. **Install the Pulley Joints**  
   - Each joint of the finger has **pin slots**.  
   - Position a pulley between the pin slots, then insert one 8 mm pin through.  
   - The fit should be tight enough that:
     - The pulley spins freely, **and**
     - The pin doesn’t fall out when the finger is tilted.  
   - Repeat for all **three joints** along the finger.  
   - These pulleys will later guide the actuation string to minimize friction during movement.

4. **Attach the Elastic Band**  
   - You should have one 8 mm pin and one 12 mm pin remaining.  
   - At the **tip of the finger**, locate the small hole on the side.  
   - Insert the **8 mm pin** through that hole and through a loop of the elastic band — this will create tension when pulled.

5. **Feed and Anchor the Nylon String**  
   - Tie the **nylon string** securely around the elastic band.  
   - Feed the string through the **holes at the base of each finger**, which connect the finger to the palm.  
   - Pull gently to check that the band tensions smoothly.

6. **Tension the Band at the Palm**  
   - Locate the **final pin slot** on the palm.  
   - Insert the **12 mm pin** through the elastic band loop here.  
   - This creates the restoring force that makes the finger return upright after being bent.

7. **Repeat for All Fingers**  
   - Repeat this entire process for all four fingers.  
   - Ensure pulleys rotate freely and that each band provides smooth, even tension.

---

**Tip:** A drop of light oil or graphite powder can help pulleys spin more smoothly and reduce long-term string wear.
