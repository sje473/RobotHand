## 🖐️ Finger Assembly Instructions

### Materials Needed
- Metal paper clips (>1 mm diameter)  
- Rubber bands (elastic)  
- Nylon string (fishing line)  
- Wire cutters or pliers  

---

### Steps

1. **Prepare the Pins**  
   - Cut **four (4)** paper clip sections to **8 mm** length.  
   - Cut **one (1)** section to **12 mm** length.  

2. **Deburr the Pulley Holes**  
   - Feed each 8 mm pin through the pulleys to clear out any 3D printing residue or tight tolerances that could create friction.  
   - Remove the pins once the pulleys move freely.

3. **Install the Pulley Joints**  
   - Each joint of the finger has **pin slots**.  
   - Position a pulley between the pin slots, then insert one 8 mm pin through.  
   - The fit should be tight enough that:
     - The pulley can spin freely, **and**
     - The pin doesn’t slide out when the finger is tilted.  
   - Repeat for all **three joints** along the finger.

4. **Attach the Elastic Band**  
   - You should have one 8 mm pin and one 12 mm pin remaining.  
   - At the **tip of the finger**, locate the small hole on the side.  
   - Place an elastic band inside the fingertip so that inserting the **8 mm pin** through the hole also passes **through the band**.  
   - When pulled, this will tension the band.

5. **Feed and Anchor the Nylon String**  
   - Tie your **nylon string** securely around the elastic band.  
   - Feed the string through the **holes at the base of each finger** — these holes connect the finger to the palm.  
   - Pull gently to verify that the elastic band tensions properly through the same path.

6. **Tension the Band at the Palm**  
   - At the palm, locate the **final pin slot**.  
   - Use the **12 mm pin** here.  
   - Position the elastic band so that this pin passes through its loop.  
   - This provides the tension that makes the finger return to an upright (rest) position.

7. **Repeat for All Fingers**  
   - Follow the same procedure for each of the **four fingers**.  
   - Ensure that each pulley rotates freely and each band provides smooth, even tension.

---

**Tip:** A drop of light machine oil or graphite powder can further reduce friction in pulley joints.
