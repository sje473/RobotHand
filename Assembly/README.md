# 🧠 Assembly Overview

Welcome to the **Assembly Section** of the Robot Hand project!

Before diving into any specific component assembly, make sure to follow each section **in the correct order** — each step builds on the last.  
This will ensure proper alignment, tension, and motion throughout the final assembly.

---

## 🔧 Recommended Assembly Order

1. **[Finger Assembly](./fingerAssembly.md)**  
   Begin by assembling all four fingers, installing the pulleys, tensioning the elastic bands, and threading the base holes to the palm.

2. **[Thumb Assembly](./thumbAssembly.md)**  
   Assemble the thumb and its anchor piece. Similar to the fingers but with minor differences in pulley locations and tensioning setup.

3. **[Servo Mounting & Actuation String](./servoMounting.md)**  
   Mount all servos to the palm, route the wires, and connect nylon strings to each finger and thumb.  
   This connects the mechanical assembly to the actuation system.

4. **[Power Setup](./powerSetup.md)**  
   Wire your power source, ensuring safe 5V power distribution for all servos and the control board.

5. **[Arduino Cover + Floor Assembly](./coverAndFloor.md)**  
   Final structural step — install the cover plates, secure the Arduino, and tidy up your wiring to complete the build.

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes
- Follow the order **exactly** — skipping steps can make tensioning or alignment difficult later.  
- Each guide includes part-specific notes, hardware requirements, and tips for smoother motion.  
- Ensure all pulleys rotate freely before connecting strings or applying power.

---

Once complete, your hand will be fully assembled and ready for calibration and testing.

Happy building! 🦾
